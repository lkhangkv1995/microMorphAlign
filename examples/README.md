# Toy images

`image1.tiff` and `image2.tiff` are reduced-resolution, paired microscopy examples
from the MorphAlign v2 demo. They represent different imaging modalities and
are **not guaranteed** to be perfectly registered. They are included solely
for exercising the end-to-end global-plus-local workflow.

The APIs accept grayscale `H×W` and arbitrary-channel `H×W×C` TIFF images.
For your own data, change `IMAGE1_PATH` and `IMAGE2_PATH` in the notebook.
